# Statement of Work: Discovery & System Readiness

**Prepared for:** Calicut Bio Medicals  
**Date:** March 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
The objective of this Paid Discovery Phase is to lay the strategic and technical foundation for a performance-driven, Agentic CRM workflow designed explicitly for your MVP sales staff. 

Before committing to significant software development (such as the proposed WhatsApp Engagement Tier), this highly focused diagnostic phase allows us to:
1. **Shadow your top performers** to understand their immediate friction points and document exactly how they sell in the field.
2. **Evaluate your infrastructural readiness** to determine if your current CRM is technically capable of supporting modern, headless AI-driven workflows safely and reliably.
3. **Establish the Baseline Metrics** (e.g., current time-to-quote, lead-to-close velocity) required to structure a mutually beneficial, outcome-based success fee for the ultimate build phase.

This phase completely eliminates the risk of building complex integrations on an incompatible legacy system by delivering a definitive technical roadmap first.

---

## 2. Scope of Work
This engagement will span approximately **Two (2) Weeks** and encompass two core diagnostic activities:

### Phase A: Workflow Shadowing & System Evaluation
* **Field Shadowing:** Conduct deep-dive shadowing sessions (three half-days, approx. 4 hours each) covering up to 3 of your identified MVP sales staff. Document exact daily workflows, identify administrative bottlenecks, and map decision-making processes.
* **Architecture Audit:** Analyze the technical capabilities of your primary CRM tool (specifically its APIs, database accessibility, and webhooks).
* **Feasibility Ruling (Migration Pivot):** Determine definitively if the existing CRM backend can securely integrate with a modern interface. If the system is deemed structurally inadequate for your future AI goals, this phase will formally identify that a CRM platform migration is required.
* **Agent Selection:** Identify the single highest-ROI opportunity for your first automated agentic workflow (assuming the CRM is viable).

### Phase B: Technical & Data Readiness Assessment
* **Data Baseline (To Prove ROI):** We need to extract current task performance data from your old CRM interface to establish the benchmark. We must prove how slow the current process is in order to quantify the time saved by the new AI-driven workflows (regardless of the final interface chosen).
* **Data Hygiene Audit (For AI Reliability):** Any AI agent or automation we build—whether it's a WhatsApp bot or a different custom interface—will still rely on your CRM as its backend database. We must determine if the CRM's current data is clean and structured enough for an AI to read/write accurately without hallucinating or pulling incorrect records.

---

## 3. Deliverables
At the conclusion of this Discovery Phase, Calicut Bio Medicals will be provided with the following actionable assets:

1. **The Workflow Map & CRM Evaluation:** A detailed teardown of the MVP sales process highlighting friction points, alongside a definitive technical recommendation on whether your current CRM infrastructure is adequate for Tier-4 Agentic interfaces.
2. **The Baseline Report:** The documented historical performance metrics that will act as the undeniable benchmark for our future outcome-based build contract.
3. **The Implementation Roadmap:** A concrete, data-backed estimate for the next phase. If your CRM passes the audit, this will quote the development of your first custom Agentic workflow. If the CRM fails, this will outline the strategic roadmap and cost for migrating to a modern, AI-ready CRM platform.

## 5. Assumptions & Scope Constraints
To ensure massive value delivery within this fixed timeframe and budget, this agreement is governed by the following constraints:
* **The "MVP-First" Mandate:** This entire engagement is predicated on the strategic directive to maximize the capabilities and efficiency of your most valuable (MVP) sales staff. The CRM evaluation is not based on general organizational use, but strictly on whether the system serves these top performers.
* **Scope Boundary:** The technical evaluation is strictly limited to the primary CRM tool currently actively used by the sales team.
* **Shadowing Cap:** Workflow shadowing is limited to a maximum of three (3) half-day sessions covering no more than three (3) specific MVP sales members.
* **No Code Clause:** This engagement is entirely strategic and diagnostic. It does **not** include the coding, deployment, or testing of a working software prototype or AI agent at this stage.
* **Client Obligations:** The client agrees to provide timely access to necessary CRM admin environments, API documentation, and performance data required to complete the evaluation.

---

## 6. Confidentiality & Data Security
We recognize that analyzing your CRM involves access to highly sensitive customer and operational data. 
* **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA):** A standard MNDA will be executed by both parties prior to the commencement of Phase A.
* **Data Handling:** No customer data will be downloaded, scraped, or stored on local, unencrypted servers during the Data Hygiene Audit. All analysis will be performed within the secure sandbox environments provided by Calicut Bio Medicals.

---

## 7. Next Steps: Strategic Roadmap & Execution
Upon the successful completion of this Discovery sprint, we will review the findings together. Based on factual data—rather than assumptions—we will determine the correct strategic path forward:

* **Pathway A (The CRM is Viable):** We will mutually agree on the highest-value agentic workflow to build out as a working Proof of Concept (e.g., the WhatsApp Voice bot). That development sprint will be formally scoped and estimated.
* **Pathway B (The CRM is Inadequate):** If the audit reveals that your current system cannot safely or efficiently support these MVP-centric goals, the next step will not be a PoC. Instead, we will provide a comprehensive roadmap, strategy, and cost estimate for migrating your sales team to a modern, AI-ready CRM platform.

Both pathways ensure your investment is completely protected, moving your MVP sales force relentlessly toward maximum efficiency.
