Statement of Work

Discovery & System Readiness

Prepared for: Calicut Bio Medicals

Date: March 2026

Executive Summary & Objective

Lay the strategic and technical foundation for a performance-driven CRM workflow designed for your Top-Performing sales staff.

  • Clarity Before Commitment: Optimize current CRM investment before considering structural changes to eliminate the risk of building on incompatible legacy systems.
  • Identify Friction: Assess usability, adoption barriers, and informal workflows (like WhatsApp & Excel) to align improvements with actual sales behavior.
  • Evidence-Based Choices: Determine if current CRM is technically capable of supporting modern, AI-driven workflows safely.

Scope of Work: 2-Week Diagnostic

Phase A: Workflow & System

  • Field Shadowing: 3 half-day sessions with your top performers to map daily workflows & bottlenecks.
  • Capability Assessment: Analyze CRM APIs, database, and webhooks for future enhancement readiness.

Phase B: Data Readiness

  • Data Baseline: Quantify current time-to-quote and process performance to prove future ROI.
  • Hygiene Audit: Ensure CRM data is clean/structured for AI to read accurately without hallucinating.
Focus: Top Performers only. Read-only access. No active coding or data changes.

The Deliverables

A written report and review session with leadership detailing:

  • Workflow Map & CRM Evaluation: Teardown of top-performer friction and a definitive technical recommendation on CRM infrastructure adequacy.
  • Prioritized Improvement Matrix: Sequenced action plan for usability fixes, integrations, and structural considerations.
  • Baseline Performance Metrics: Historical benchmarks for outcome-based building.
  • Implementation Roadmap: Concrete, data-backed estimate for either an Agentic workflow POC, interface layering, or a hybrid architecture.

Project Investment & Framework

Investment

  • Total: ₹49,000 (excl. taxes)
  • Structure: Fixed-fee diagnostic
  • Terms: 50% start / 50% delivery

Data Security

  • Full Mutual NDA executed up front
  • Live environment, strict Read-Only access
  • No data downloaded or stored locally
Provides absolute cost certainty while validating tech and baselines.

Client Obligations & Timelines

  • Access: Provide read-only CRM access 3–5 days prior to shadowing.
  • Availability: Ensure timely access to Top-Performing staff for mandatory shadowing sessions.
  • Vendor Collaboration: Facilitate required technical discussions with the current CRM vendor.

Note: The 2-week timeline is contingent upon vendor responsiveness and shadowing availability. Delays will pause and proportionally extend the schedule.

Next Steps: Strategic Execution

Post-Discovery, we determine the correct strategic path using factual data:

  • Pathway A (CRM is Viable): We mutually agree on the highest-value workflow to build as a working Proof of Concept (e.g., WhatsApp Voice bot) with a formal scope.
  • Pathway B (CRM is Inadequate): We execute a new vendor selection based on a comprehensive roadmap to migrate your sales team to a modern, AI-ready platform.
Both pathways ensure your investment is completely protected.
