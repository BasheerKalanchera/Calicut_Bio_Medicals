# Messages for the Cabio team

Harness review item 5 (Basheer, 2026-11-27). Built from the 13 Nov and
26 Nov corrections: notes not paste-ready, a note missing an Opportunity,
a sheet in a format he can't send, client data saved in the project (P110).

## Format
- Ready to send as it stands: plain text he can paste into WhatsApp
  (short lines, *bold* with stars, no tables or Markdown headings), or a
  one-page PDF when it needs a table. Never a .md file. *(13 Nov, 26 Nov)*
- Say who it is for at the top of the draft (not in the message), and
  keep one message per person or group.

## Content
- Cover everything the reader owns: before showing it, check that every
  Opportunity, customer or product the reader is responsible for is in
  it, counted from the data. *(13 Nov)*
- Each figure says where it came from in the draft's note to Basheer
  (UAT read + date), not in the message.
- Questions are numbered, one ask each, so replies can say "2: yes".
- Use the app's words (Opportunity, customer, stage names as on screen).

## Client data stays out of the project
- Anything built from UAT or Cabio's own sheets goes to the session
  scratchpad by full path, never into `docs/` or the repo, and is never
  staged. Shared case studies are anonymised (Case A, Case B). *(26 Nov, P110)*

## Before it goes
- Show the full text in chat first; Basheer's wording wins — when he
  rewrites a part, use his words as they are.
- He sends it; Claude never does. Afterwards, one Progress-Archive line:
  what was sent, to whom, the scratchpad file, and what answers to wait for.
