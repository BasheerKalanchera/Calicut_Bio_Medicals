# Cabio Sales OS — UAT Access Setup (Laptop & Mobile)

**Purpose:** step-by-step instructions to follow along live during tonight's
orientation session on Google Meet — set this up on your own laptop and/or
phone while Basheer walks through the app.

**UAT URL and your login (email + password) are in the WhatsApp group
message** — this document is the setup steps only, sent as an attachment in
the same group.

Works identically on laptop and phone — no app store, no separate download.
On mobile it can also be installed as an app icon on the home screen (see
below); on laptop it runs as a normal browser tab, or can be installed as a
desktop app the same way.

**Login:** use the email and password from the WhatsApp group message. Each
person has their own account — do not share logins.

**Opening this document:** tap it in WhatsApp to open it, same as any other
file WhatsApp sends you. Once it's open, use the URL from the group message
(not a link inside this document) for the steps below — see the in-app
browser note under your platform, since tapping a link from inside WhatsApp
or its document viewer can behave differently than tapping it in your
regular browser.

---

## Laptop / Desktop

1. Open **Chrome** or **Edge** and go to the UAT URL from the WhatsApp
   group message.
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. That's it — no install required. Keep the tab open, or bookmark it.
4. **Optional — install as a desktop app:** in Chrome/Edge, click the install
   icon in the address bar (a small monitor-with-arrow icon, right side of
   the address bar) → **Install**. This opens the app in its own window
   without browser tabs/toolbars, and adds a shortcut to your desktop/start
   menu.

---

## Mobile — Android (Chrome)

**Since you're getting the link from WhatsApp,** tapping it directly will
likely open WhatsApp's own built-in browser instead of Chrome — the
Install option won't be there in that view. Tap the **⋮** or **share**
icon in that in-app browser first and choose **Open in Chrome**, then
continue below.

1. Open the UAT URL from the WhatsApp group message in **Chrome** (see
   note above).
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right) → **Install app** (or you may see an
   "Add to Home screen" banner appear automatically — tap **Install** on
   that instead).
4. Confirm. The Cabio icon appears on your home screen / app drawer.
5. Launch from that icon going forward — opens full-screen, no browser
   address bar, like a regular app.

## Mobile — iPhone (Safari)

**Must use Safari** — Add to Home Screen only works from Safari on iOS, not
Chrome.

**Since you're getting the link from WhatsApp,** tapping it directly will
likely open WhatsApp's own built-in browser instead of Safari — the Add to
Home Screen option won't be there in that view. Tap the **share** or
**•••** icon in that in-app browser first and choose **Open in Safari**,
then continue below.

1. Open the UAT URL from the WhatsApp group message in **Safari** (see
   note above).
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an upward arrow, bottom toolbar).
4. **Scroll down** and tap **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
5. The Cabio icon appears on your Home Screen.
6. Launch from that icon going forward — opens full-screen, no Safari
   address bar, like a regular app.

---

## Notes

- First load after a period of inactivity may take a few extra seconds —
  the backend server briefly "wakes up." This should be rare during active
  use, but if a screen seems to hang on first load, wait ~30 seconds and
  retry before assuming something's wrong.
- This is a test environment (UAT) with a fresh, small dataset — some
  accounts/hospitals you work with may not be in the system yet. Add them
  as you go; that's expected and part of what this session is for.
- If login fails or a screen shows an unexpected error, note exactly what
  you did and what the error said, and flag it during the session rather
  than retrying repeatedly — it may be something worth fixing on the spot.
- If the installed app looks outdated or stuck after an update, fully close
  it (swipe it away from your recent-apps list) and reopen from the home
  screen icon — this clears a stale cached version.
