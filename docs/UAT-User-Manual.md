# Cabio Sales OS — UAT User Manual

Welcome to the Cabio Sales OS UAT! This system is designed specifically to help you achieve your sales targets and manage your pipeline efficiently.

*Note: Some features are restricted based on your role (e.g., Sales Representative vs. Manager).*

---

## 1. Global Quick Actions (All Roles)
Available at all times in the top right corner of your screen.

### ➕ Lead (Quick Lead Modal)
Use this when you need to quickly capture a new opportunity on the go.
1. Click **+ Lead**.
2. Select the **Customer Account** (or enter a new hospital name).
3. Fill in the required requirement details.
4. The opportunity will automatically drop into your Pipeline for follow-up later.

### ➕ Log (Log Activity Modal)
Use this to log visits, calls, and updates immediately after they happen.
1. Click **+ Log**.
2. Select the Account, Project, or Opportunity this activity is related to.
3. Add notes about the interaction and set your **Next Action Date** if follow-up is required.

> **Next Action is mandatory.** Except for internal Manager Notes, every activity you log requires a Next Action (what you'll do next, and by when). This isn't optional — the form won't submit without it. It's by design: every customer interaction should leave a trail of what happens next, not just a record of what happened.

## 2. Navigation & Sidebar
Click the **☰ (Menu)** icon in the top left to open your sidebar navigation. From here you can access:
* **Account Management**: View Customers and Projects.
* **Pipeline**: Manage your active opportunities.
* **Next Actions**: View your upcoming tasks and reminders.
* **Product Catalog**: Browse the full company product line — available to everyone, not just Managers/Admins.
* *(Managers/Admins only)* **User Directory**.

At the bottom of the sidebar, you'll see your **Profile Card**, which confirms your current Role, Zone, and active SBU (Strategic Business Unit).

> **Tip:** Customers, Projects, and the Product Catalog each have a search box at the top of their list — use it to jump straight to a record instead of scrolling. Results update automatically as you type; there's no need to press Enter or click a search button.

---

## 3. Account Management
*Navigation: Sidebar > Account Management*

This section is split into two tabs:
* **Customers Tab**: Displays a directory of all hospitals and clinics. 
  * Click "+ Add" to create a new Account.
  * Click on any Account to open its **Customer 360** view.
* **Projects Tab**: Groups multiple opportunities under a single strategic initiative (e.g., a "Hospital Expansion" or a "Government Tender"). 
  * Click "+ Add" to create a new Project.
  * Click on a Project to open its **Project 360** view (Section 5).

---

## 4. Customer 360 View
*Navigation: Click on any Account from the Customers list*

This is your deep-dive view into a specific hospital or clinic. It contains several sub-tabs:
* **Overview**: Basic hospital details and primary contacts.
* **Activity**: Full history of visits, calls, and interactions logged against this account.
* **Stakeholders**: Crucial for tracking relationships (e.g., Dr. Ahmed, Purchase Manager). You can track their influence level and Net Promoter Score (NPS) here.
* **Projects**: Active strategic initiatives at this hospital. Click any Project card to jump straight to its detail view.
* **Opportunities**: All active and closed deals specific to this hospital. Click any Opportunity card to jump straight to its detail view.
* **Installed Base**: Equipment this hospital already owns — useful context before pitching an upgrade or a competing product.

---

## 5. Project 360 View
*Navigation: Click on any Project from the Projects tab (Section 3), or from a Customer 360's Projects tab*

* **Project Details**: Read-only summary — Account, Status, Owner, and Bid Submission Date. Click **Edit** to change any of these.
* **Opportunities**: All deals linked to this Project. Click **+ Add** to create a new Opportunity for this Project directly. Click any Opportunity card to jump straight to its detail view, or use Edit to update it without leaving this screen.
* **Activity**: Log and review visits, calls, and interactions tied to this Project directly.

---

## 6. Pipeline Management (Sales Reps)
*Navigation: Sidebar > Pipeline*

The Pipeline is your primary workspace for driving revenue.
* **Stages**: Opportunities move from left to right through 7 stages: **Lead → Qualified → Demo → Clinical Evaluation → Negotiation → Order → Delivery & Installation**.
* **Moving Deals**: You can drag and drop opportunities between columns as they progress through the sales cycle. Some stage moves require certain fields to already be filled in (e.g. a Lead Source) — if a move is blocked, the app will tell you what's missing.
* **Search**: Use the search box at the top to find a deal by opportunity name or hospital name — results filter instantly as you type.
* **Filters**: Use the filters at the top to focus on specific stages, owners, or expected closing dates.

### Opportunity Detail View
*Navigation: Click on any Opportunity card in the Pipeline*
Inside an opportunity, you'll find several sub-tabs:
* **Overview**: Core deal fields — Stage, Status, Owner, Win Probability (you can manually override this based on field intelligence), and key dates.
* **Activity**: Log and review visits, calls, and interactions specific to this deal.
* **Next Actions**: Follow-up tasks tied to this opportunity. See Section 7 for how these work.
* **Products**: The equipment/items included in this deal. Once you add Products here, the Opportunity's value is calculated automatically from them (quantity × price − discount) — it replaces any manually-entered estimate.
* **Splits**: How revenue credit for this deal is divided among contributors. Splits must always add up to 100%; by default the creator gets 100% until adjusted. Only teammates in the same SBU as the Opportunity can be added as a split participant.
* **Stakeholders**: The contacts (linked from the Account) actually involved in this specific deal.

## 7. Next Actions
*Navigation: Sidebar > Next Actions*

This screen helps you plan your coverage and daily execution.
* Displays a list of all your upcoming reminders and scheduled tasks.
* Clicking on a reminder will take you directly to the related Account or Opportunity so you can execute the task immediately.
* **Completing a Next Action**: click the reminder's Complete action, then describe what you actually did — this closing note is mandatory, since every follow-up needs a record of how it was resolved. If that resolution itself surfaces a new follow-up, you can add one right there before saving — it'll appear as a new Next Action.

---

## 8. Product Catalog
*Navigation: Sidebar > Product Catalog*
* Browse the full company product line — every product from every SBU is visible to everyone, so you always know what Cabio offers even outside your own SBU.
* Filter by SBU using the buttons at the top.
* You can only *add* a product to an Opportunity if it belongs to that Opportunity's own SBU — browsing is company-wide, but adding to a deal stays SBU-scoped.

### User Directory
*Navigation: Sidebar > User Directory*
* Manage access for the team, assign roles, and allocate users to specific Zones and SBUs.
