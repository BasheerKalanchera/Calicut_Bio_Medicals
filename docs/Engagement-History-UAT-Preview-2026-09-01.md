# Engagement History — Full UAT Preview (all accounts with logged activity)

**What this is:** a manual dry-run of the weekly auto-summary feature, done by
hand against every one of the 58 UAT accounts that has at least one logged
Activity. This is what a rep would see on the "Engagement History" tab with
**zero extra effort on their part** — every summary below is built entirely
from Calls/Visits/Meetings/Emails they already logged through the existing
+Log button, plus their existing Next Action / reminder entries. No new
field, no new note type.

**Sorted by activity count, most first** — to make the point visible at a
glance: the more a rep logs on an account, the sharper and more current the
picture gets.

**Format:** each account gets one summary in six parts — where it currently
stands, who the key people are, what they care about, what's blocking, the
rep's own narrative "what's next," and now also the **actual Next Action
logged in the system** (the same reminder shown on the Next Actions tab),
with its due date and whether it's overdue. Where the log doesn't say
something, it's marked "not specified" rather than invented.

**Data note:** this UAT environment is live — while pulling this data,
7 accounts picked up a brand-new activity dated today (2026-09-01), all
logged by Haroon in real time. Those 7 are refreshed below.

Intended to show Haroon (and the team) what they get back for activities
they're already entering — 19 of these 58 accounts are his.

## On the "manager dashboard" idea

Yes — this is a genuinely good fit for one, and worth pursuing separately
from the rep-facing Engagement History tab. The account-level summary
answers "where does this deal stand," which is what a rep needs; a manager
needs the aggregate view instead — the system-wide snapshot at the top of
this document (58/79 activity coverage, 59% of Next Actions overdue, 10
accounts with activity but no Opportunity) is exactly manager-dashboard
shaped, not account-page shaped. The one thing worth deciding before
building it: this exercise also surfaced real data-quality noise (a "Nil"
reminder, a truncated "Foll," an exact duplicate) — fine for a rep reading
their own account, but a manager-facing rollup should probably filter or
flag junk entries rather than let them skew a team-wide stat. Worth a
short, separate scoping conversation rather than folding into this plan.
